Question: Do our studies agree on where to place the primary CTA?
------------------------------------------------------------

ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------
Based on the research, the studies show **device-specific differences** rather than full agreement:

**Mobile users** prefer the primary CTA above the fold [f-b9016884]. Users completed checkout faster and felt more confident when the button was visible without scrolling.

**Tablet users** behave differently - they consistently scrolled past above-the-fold CTAs and only engaged with the primary action when it was placed below the fold near the order total [f-9ec44e6f].

These findings don't contradict each other because they address different device contexts. The optimal CTA placement depends on the device type, with mobile favoring above-the-fold and tablet favoring placement near the order summary below the fold.
