Based on my search of the research corpus, here's what we know about BlueCart's checkout:

## Guest Checkout & Account Creation
Major friction exists with guest checkout visibility. First-time users struggle to find the guest option across devices [f-94a7bda3] [f-b053f3af], with the issue most severe on tablets where the horizontal layout breaks down [f-10c15b23]. Users feel forced to create accounts when they want to make a single purchase [f-a92fd98a].

## Pricing & Transparency
Shipping costs appearing for the first time on the payment screen causes significant negative reactions, with 9 of 12 participants expressing frustration and 3 stating they would abandon the purchase [f-26d7ccf1]. Users feel uncertain about what they paid when totals change at the moment of purchase, worrying about double-charging [f-8e71b427].

## Promo Codes
The promo code feature has two key issues: discoverability and feedback. Users miss the promo code link entirely because it resembles footer text rather than an interactive element [f-4274472b] [f-1993bdca]. When codes are applied, the lack of success state confirmation causes users to re-enter codes or assume they failed [f-c7aabd07].

## CTA Placement
CTA placement preferences differ by device. Mobile users complete checkout faster with the primary button above the fold [f-b9016884], while tablet users consistently scroll past above-the-fold CTAs and only engage when the button is near the order total below the fold [f-9ec44e6f].

## Other Issues
- Touch targets on tablets use 24px instead of the 44px minimum, causing mis-taps and accidental item removal [f-e1c1aeb9]
- New users want security and trust indicators before entering payment information [f-cc5e472e]
