Question: What's the most severe issue users had with checkout?
------------------------------------------------------------

ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------
Based on the search results, the most severe checkout issues all have "high" severity ratings. The most critical appear to be:

1. **Uncertainty about order completion and double-charging** - Users didn't know if their order went through when the total changed at purchase [f-8e71b427], which creates significant anxiety about being charged incorrectly.

2. **Hidden guest checkout option** - Multiple findings show that 5 of 8 participants (on mobile) [f-b053f3af] and 5 of 7 participants (on tablet) [f-94a7bda3] failed to find guest checkout and attempted to back out, which directly causes checkout abandonment.

3. **Late shipping cost disclosure** - 9 of 12 participants reacted negatively when shipping costs appeared only on the payment screen, with 3 explicitly stating they would have abandoned the purchase [f-26d7ccf1].

The order completion uncertainty [f-8e71b427] appears to be the most severe as it creates post-purchase anxiety and potential distrust, while the hidden guest checkout issues [f-b053f3af] [f-94a7bda3] are the most widespread, affecting the majority of users and causing actual abandonment attempts.
